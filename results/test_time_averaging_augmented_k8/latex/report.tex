\title{Random Sign Augmentation Beats Canonicalization\\
       for Laplacian Positional Encodings on ogbg-molpcba}
\author{ogbg-molpcba experiments, GIN backbone}
\date{\today}

\begin{document}
\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
On ogbg-molpcba with a 5-layer GIN and $k\!\in\!\{3,8,16\}$ Laplacian
eigenvectors as positional encoding, random sign-flip augmentation
(\texttt{random\_augmented}) outperforms deterministic canonicalization
(\texttt{map} and \texttt{oap}) at $8$ of $9$ tested $(k,h)$ cells. The
advantage grows with both $k$ and $h$: at $k\!=\!16$,
\texttt{random\_augmented} beats \texttt{oap} by $+0.0031$ to $+0.0086$
test AP and \texttt{map} by $+0.0081$ to $+0.0137$.
\end{abstract}

%==============================================================================
\section{Setup}
%==============================================================================

Three approaches to the sign + basis ambiguity of LapPE:
\begin{itemize}\itemsep0pt
\item \texttt{map} --- maximum-absolute-position canonicalization (deterministic).
\item \texttt{oap} --- ordered-absolute-position canonicalization (deterministic).
\item \texttt{random\_augmented} --- training-time random sign flips per
      eigenvector per epoch; the network learns to be invariant to the
      sign ambiguity.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Hyperparameters.} GIN, $5$ layers, batch $32$, lr $10^{-3}$,
Adam + cosine schedule. Hidden dim $h\!\in\!\{16, 128, 512\}$. Seeds
$\{0,1,2\}$. Eigval-scaling off. Cache: $15$ eigenvectors. PyTorch~2.10,
CUDA~12.8, conda env \texttt{schnet-angle}.

%==============================================================================
\section{Bottom line}
%==============================================================================

\begin{itemize}\itemsep2pt
\item \textbf{Random sign-flip augmentation outperforms canonicalization
      on ogbg-molpcba with GIN at every $(k, h)$ tested except
      $k\!=\!3$/$h\!=\!512$.}
\item The advantage grows with $k$: at $k\!=\!16$ the gap is
      $+0.0031$ to $+0.0086$~AP across $h\!\in\!\{16,128,512\}$ versus
      \texttt{oap}, and $+0.0081$ to $+0.0137$ versus \texttt{map}.
\item \textbf{Practical recipe} for ogbg-molpcba-like data with
      LapPE: prefer \texttt{random\_augmented} with $k\!\geq\!8$ and
      hidden dim $\geq\!128$.
\end{itemize}
