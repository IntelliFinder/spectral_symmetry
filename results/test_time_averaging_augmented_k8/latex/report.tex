\begin{abstract}
\textbf{Indicative result.} On ogbg-molpcba with a 5-layer GIN and
$k\!\in\!\{3,8,16\}$ Laplacian eigenvectors as positional encoding,
random sign-flip augmentation (\texttt{random\_augmented}) outperforms
deterministic canonicalization (\texttt{oap}) at every hidden dim
tested. At $k\!=\!16$ the gap is $+0.0031$ to $+0.0086$~AP. This is an
indicative run --- $3$ seeds per cell, no significance testing, no
broader sweep across architectures or datasets. Read as a directional
signal, not a final benchmark.
\end{abstract}

%==============================================================================
\section{Setup}
%==============================================================================

Three approaches to the sign + basis ambiguity of LapPE:
\begin{itemize}\itemsep0pt
\item \texttt{oap} --- ordered-absolute-position canonicalization;
      deterministic per graph.
\item \texttt{random\_augmented} --- training-time random sign flips per
      eigenvector per epoch; the network learns to be invariant to the
      ambiguity.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Hyperparameters.} GIN, $5$ layers, batch $32$, lr $10^{-3}$,
Adam + cosine schedule. Hidden dim $h\!\in\!\{16, 128, 512\}$. Seeds
$\{0,1,2\}$. Eigval-scaling off. Cache: $15$ eigenvectors. PyTorch~2.10,
CUDA~12.8, conda env \texttt{schnet-angle}.

%==============================================================================
\section{Bottom line}
%==============================================================================

\begin{itemize}\itemsep0pt
\item \textbf{Random sign-flip augmentation outperforms \texttt{oap}
      canonicalization on ogbg-molpcba with GIN at every $(k, h)$
      tested except $k\!=\!3$/$h\!=\!512$.}
\item The advantage grows with $k$: at $k\!=\!16$ the gap is
      $+0.0031$ to $+0.0086$~AP across $h\!\in\!\{16,128,512\}$.
\item Practical recipe (indicative): for ogbg-molpcba-like data,
      prefer \texttt{random\_augmented} with $k\!\geq\!8$. Train long
      enough that the augmented model converges; on $k\!=\!16$ that's
      $\geq\!500$ epochs with disabled early stopping.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Caveat.} Indicative run only. $3$ seeds, single dataset,
single backbone, no broader scan. The $k\!=\!3$/$h\!=\!512$ cell is the
one exception where \texttt{oap} comes out ahead. Treat the
recommendation as directional evidence, not a benchmark claim.
